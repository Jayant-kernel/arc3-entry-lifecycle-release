# Scope of the project grant

The accompanying MIT-0 licence is adopted for original contributions created
for this project, only to the extent that the project's licensors hold rights
in those contributions. It is not a claim to own or relicense mixed files in
their entirety. No independent legal-title certification is asserted.

The covered contributions are the original lifecycle/budget/supervisor logic
in `entry/entry_runtime.py`, original input/source-validation and orchestration
logic in `entry/entry_worker.py`, original adaptation/diagnostics-suppression
logic in `entry/entry_diagnostics.py`, and the original outer notebook wrapper.
Original release documentation and `verify_release.py` are also covered.

Excluded material retains its applicable inherited terms: upstream source
substrings embedded in diagnostics; Franzen policy cells and `entry_policy.py`;
copied/adapted upstream portions; harness patches; embedded third-party PAYLOAD
content; TAAF/framework source; model assets; vendor libraries/wheels; data and
existing notices. Any portion of uncertain provenance is excluded from this
new grant. Preserve `entry/UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt` and component notices.

The basis for this decision is the documented project-created integration
contributions and the user's delegation of completion/licensing decisions.
That authorization permits the scoped choice; it does not manufacture rights
over excluded material. This licence does not resolve competition eligibility
or grant rights over separately acquired Qwen/NVIDIA/other dependencies.
